CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION

AI-assisted support operation

Challenge
A commerce operation received a continuous stream of customer email across multiple storefronts. Every message required manual reading, order lookups and policy decisions.
Solution
We engineered an AI workflow that reads incoming email, retrieves live order and shipment data, and prepares policy-compliant responses and next steps.
Result
Email handled in a fraction of the time, consistent with business policy, in daily use by a support team across three storefronts.
INBOX→AI PROCESSING→ORDER DATA→RESPONSE
INVENTORY & OPERATIONS

Custom inventory system

Challenge
A trading and refurbishment business needed full visibility of every product as it moved through the operation.
Solution
We built a custom inventory system that registers each product at intake and tracks condition, origin, warehouse location and sales status.
Result
One reliable source of truth for stock, location and status, replacing spreadsheets and memory.
INTAKE→CONDITION→WAREHOUSE→SALE
BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Central backoffice for a consultancy

Challenge
A consultancy ran client work, lead follow-up and outreach across disconnected tools and manual steps.
Solution
We engineered a central backoffice: one client database, AI-assisted lead response and automated outbound follow-up.
Result
Faster lead response, structured client data and an operation that scales without extra administrative work.
LEAD→AI RESPONSE→CLIENT DATA→FOLLOW-UP
PRODUCT DATA

Automated product publishing

Challenge
Publishing products required writing descriptions, size charts and advertising copy by hand for every item.
Solution
We built AI content workflows that generate product descriptions, size charts and ad copy from source data and publish it directly into the webshop.
Result
New products go live in minutes instead of hours, in a consistent tone and format.
SOURCE DATA→AI GENERATION→REVIEW→PUBLISH
